# Change Record Example

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Symptom:** Describe the observed problem.

**Evidence:** Record commands, relevant metrics, and log findings. Remove secrets and sensitive infrastructure details.

**Hypothesis:** Explain the suspected cause.

**Change:** Describe one controlled change.

**Verification:** Record how service health was confirmed.

**Rollback:** Document the reversal procedure.

**Prevention:** Record any monitoring, configuration, or process improvement.
